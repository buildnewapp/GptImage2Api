# Quick start

This guide introduces the basic Sdance AI generation workflow and helps you turn an idea into your first AI video.

> This is the first example page in the documentation center. Future guides can use the same navigation, search, and MDX content structure.

## Before you start

- Prepare a clear scene description covering the subject, action, environment, and camera treatment.
- For image-to-video generation, choose a reference image with a clear subject and stable composition.
- Sign in so you can submit generation tasks and review the results in your history.

## Generate your first video

1. Open the Sdance AI generator.
2. Choose text-to-video or image-to-video based on the material you have.
3. Select a model and configure the aspect ratio, duration, and quality you need.
4. Enter your prompt and upload a reference image when using image-to-video.
5. Review the estimated credit cost, submit the task, and wait for generation to finish.

## Prompt example

An effective prompt usually combines a subject, action, setting, lighting, and camera language:

```text
A dancer stands on a neon street after the rain as the camera slowly pushes in.
Fabric moves in the wind, cinematic lighting, crisp detail, natural fluid motion.
```

## Review the result

Check subject consistency, motion continuity, composition, and detail stability. If the result misses your intent, remove conflicting instructions and place the most important subject and action near the beginning of the prompt.
